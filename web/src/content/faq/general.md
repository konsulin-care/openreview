---
question: "What is OpenReview?"
category: "general"
order: 1
---

OpenReview is a local-first systematic literature review screening tool. It helps researchers import, deduplicate, and screen large volumes of academic papers against inclusion and exclusion criteria.

The application runs entirely on your machine. A Go engine handles data integrity and the API, while an Astro-powered web interface provides the user experience. Your data never leaves your device.
